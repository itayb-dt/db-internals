title: Database Internals
---

An open, interactive map of how databases work. Every dot on the left is a concept. Every line says what you need to understand first. The articles use **PostgreSQL** as the reference engine and note how other engines differ.

**How to use it.** Drag to rotate the map, scroll to zoom, and click a dot to read about it. A ring on a dot means there is a full article. Click a subject in the legend to hide or show it. Each article explains what a concept is, why it matters for performance, how to measure it, and what to change. To follow a path from basic to advanced, open the **Learning tracks** tab.

## About the project

The map and the articles are plain files in a Git repository. To add or fix a topic, see `CONTRIBUTING.md` in the repository.
